OpenArm Quest Teleop

Put the headset on, tap START, then follow the text you see in the headset.

Controls in the headset

  • Hold GRIP = drive that arm (release = freeze). The gripper copies where your hand is and how it is turned: hold the controller the way the gripper points
  • TRIGGER (while holding grip) = close gripper. After you grab again, the gripper waits until the trigger reaches its current opening, so a held object stays held
  • B = align (stand beside the robot facing its way, point right controller straight ahead)
  • A / X = fine mode: moves and turns 3x smaller
  • Hold RIGHT thumbstick 1 s = switch arms on
  • Y = STOP (arms lower and switch off)

Start: Power on (here) → Enter AR → the arms rise to the ready pose by themselves → B to align.
Squeeze = move arm (hold). Trigger = gripper. A / X = fine mode.
B = align: stand beside the robot facing the way it faces, point the right controller straight ahead, press B.
Y = STOP (park and power off). Arms off? Hold a thumbstick 1 s to power on again. Thumbstick click = ready pose.
